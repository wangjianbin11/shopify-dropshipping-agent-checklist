# Trade Compliance Evidence and Escalation Log

A neutral operations log for questions that must be verified before a cross-border ecommerce route is released. It records the question, evidence owner, authority consulted, decision, and review date without pretending that an old threshold or general article settles a live shipment.

## Mandatory escalation triggers

- Product classification, origin, valuation, labeling, licensing, sanctions, restricted goods, safety, tax, importer-of-record, or reporting status is unclear.
- A rule, threshold, carrier condition, or marketplace requirement may have changed since the last stored evidence.
- The commercial invoice, product description, website claim, and physical product do not agree.
- The proposed route depends on declaring a value or description that the evidence does not support.
- A supplier or logistics provider asks the team to ignore, conceal, split, or rewrite facts to obtain clearance.

## Release gate

- [ ] The current question is written narrowly enough to answer.
- [ ] The applicable destination and product are identified.
- [ ] Evidence has a retrieved date and effective date where available.
- [ ] A qualified owner made the decision; a sales or warehouse guess is not used as legal authority.
- [ ] Open questions remain on hold.
- [ ] The next review date is set for changeable rules.

## Limits

This log does not provide legal, tax, customs, or regulatory advice. It creates a traceable path for obtaining and retaining qualified route-specific decisions.
