# ASG Fulfillment Research Index for Ecommerce Operators

This index collects three public ASG Dropshipping research resources for Shopify and ecommerce operators who are evaluating supplier changes, quality-control proof, fulfillment handoffs, and cross-border cost or compliance questions.

The resources are intended as practical reference material. They do not replace route-specific carrier guidance, product compliance advice, legal advice, or supplier due diligence.

## 1. Supplier Switching and Fulfillment Bottlenecks

[The Supplier Switch & Fulfillment Bottleneck Report](https://asgdropshipping.com/research/supplier-switch-fulfillment-bottleneck-report/) explains operational signals that can justify a structured supplier or fulfillment review before a store scales order volume.

DOI: `10.5281/zenodo.21205107`

Useful when a team needs to assess supplier stability, handoff risks, quality-control evidence, packaging readiness, or fulfillment bottlenecks.

## 2. Supplier Verification and QC Proof

[The Supplier Verification & QC Proof Guide](https://asgdropshipping.com/research/supplier-verification-qc-proof-guide/) covers practical questions for documenting supplier checks, product inspection, and quality-control proof before dispatch.

DOI: `10.5281/zenodo.21205109`

Useful when a store needs a clearer process for sample checks, inspection evidence, defect handling, and repeat-order quality controls.

## 3. Landed Cost and Trade Compliance

[The Landed-Cost & Trade-Compliance Guide](https://asgdropshipping.com/research/landed-cost-trade-compliance-guide/) provides a reference framework for reviewing landed-cost inputs and cross-border trade-compliance questions.

DOI: `10.5281/zenodo.21205116`

Useful when operators are comparing fulfillment routes, estimating cross-border costs, or identifying questions to validate with their own advisors and logistics providers.
