## Pre-production checks

- [ ] Product dimensions and packed dimensions are measured, not estimated.
- [ ] Artwork, logo, copy, barcode, warnings, and language requirements have an approved version.
- [ ] The packaging material and protective function match the product risk.
- [ ] Unit cost, setup cost, plate or tooling cost, storage, assembly labor, and replacement waste are separated.
- [ ] Unused packaging ownership and disposal responsibility are written down.
- [ ] A plain-packaging fallback exists if branded stock is late or defective.

## Expansion gate

- [ ] The approved packaging version matches the live pilot.
- [ ] Packaging defects and product-damage observations are reviewed.
- [ ] Packed dimensions did not create an unplanned freight tier.
- [ ] Assembly time and storage space are included in the economics.
- [ ] Remaining design, compliance, or durability questions have named owners.
- [ ] The expansion quantity is based on observed pilot demand, not only supplier MOQ pressure.

## Limits

A low initial quantity reduces inventory exposure but does not remove setup, quality, compliance, or operational risk. Product and destination requirements still need specific review.
