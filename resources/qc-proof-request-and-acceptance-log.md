# QC Proof Request and Acceptance Log

A blank log for defining what quality-control proof is required, what was received, and who accepted or rejected it before dispatch. Use it at SKU and order level; do not treat a chat message saying "checked" as evidence.

## Minimum acceptance gate

- [ ] The evidence resolves to the intended SKU, order, batch, or unit.
- [ ] The current specification version is named.
- [ ] The requested angle, measurement, function, label, accessory, or packaging checkpoint is visible or recorded.
- [ ] The original file opens and its capture time is retained.
- [ ] A named person made the acceptance decision.
- [ ] Failed or incomplete evidence did not silently become a pass.
- [ ] Reinspection created a new record instead of overwriting the failure.
- [ ] Dispatch release points to the accepted QC record.

## When photo or video is not enough

Escalate when the claim depends on a measurement, calibration, destructive test, laboratory result, safety standard, or legal requirement that ordinary warehouse media cannot establish. Record the qualified evidence required and keep the order on hold until that path closes.

## Limits

Media can document selected conditions at a point in time. It does not prove every unit is defect-free and does not replace product-specific safety, regulatory, or laboratory requirements.
